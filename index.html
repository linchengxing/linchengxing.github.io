About

I am a master's student in Computer Technology at the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC), advised by Prof. Wen Li. I received my bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Technology from Guangdong University of Technology (GDUT).

I work on embodied AI and 3D vision, with a focus on vision-and-language navigation, spatial understanding, test-time training, and point cloud representation learning.

Research Interests

  • Navigation & embodied AI: vision-and-language navigation, spatial understanding, and adaptive memory.
  • Learning & adaptation: test-time training, in-context learning, and self-supervised learning.
  • 3D vision: point cloud understanding and efficient representation learning.
